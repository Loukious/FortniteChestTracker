Opened includes normal openings and destroyed unopened chests attributed to players in the replay. A chest that resets and is opened or destroyed again counts again; repeated updates within the same opening count once. Counts are the minimum confirmed by the recording. + means more chests may be missing, especially from distant teammates. Unknown means unavailable data; totals exclude it.

Reward counts include recorded observations in each replay. Nearby players outside your squad appear as Other player in Rewards when their reward is recorded. None recorded does not mean no reward was received. Treasure receipts without a prize ID appear as Treasure (unknown type); they may include Gizmos or shared teammate rewards.
